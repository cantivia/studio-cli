# Credits

Studio CLI stands on other people's work.

- **[Pixel Agents](https://github.com/pixel-agents-hq/pixel-agents)** by Pablo De Lucca (MIT). The Agent View is its webview, vendored in `vendor/pixel-agents/` with a Studio connection in place of its editor extension. Its licence is kept at `vendor/pixel-agents/LICENSE`. Its characters are based on the work of JIK-A-4 (Metro City), and the Agent View also bundles the *FS Pixel Sans Unicode* font, both exactly as upstream ships them. If you republish the Agent View assets, check their terms with the upstream project.
- **[cmux](https://github.com/manaflow-ai/cmux)**, the inspiration for vertical workspaces, notification rings and agent-aware terminals.
- **[xterm.js](https://xtermjs.org)**, **[node-pty](https://github.com/microsoft/node-pty)**, **[Electron](https://www.electronjs.org)**, **[React](https://react.dev)**, **[Vite](https://vite.dev)**, **[Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com)**, **[framer-motion](https://www.framer.com/motion/)**, **[zustand](https://zustand.docs.pmnd.rs)**, **[qrcode](https://github.com/soldair/node-qrcode)**, and **[cloudflared](https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflared)** (bundled in the Mac app to reach your phone). Each is under its own open-source licence.
