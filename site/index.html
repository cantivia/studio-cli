What it does

Built for people who live in the terminal.

Everything you already run, laid out so you can see what’s working, what’s finished, and what needs you.

Workspaces that tile themselves

One workspace per project: terminals, Claude and a browser, laid out automatically and colour-coded so you never lose your place.

Know when Claude is done

A ring and a “Done” tick when a Claude turn finishes, with its last reply right in the sidebar. Reusable workflows in one click.

Your Mac, from your phone

Scan a code once and your phone can reach your terminals anywhere, over your own private address. Remove any device, any time.

Share with a link

Send a teammate a conversation to continue in their own Claude, or let them watch a workspace live. Turn the link off whenever you like.

Install

Three steps, about a minute.

No terminal commands, no accounts.

Download

Get the file for your Mac. We pick the right one for you, or choose below.

Drag to Applications

Open the download and drag Studio CLI onto Applications, then open it. macOS will ask you to approve it once (see the FAQ).

Open a workspace

Pick a folder and start a shell, Claude, or both. Want it on your phone? Settings → Phone & remote access → Turn on.

Security

Your terminals never leave your Mac.

Studio runs on your own computer. Nothing you type or see is sent to us, and there’s no account to create or password to leak.

  • Phones pair with a one-time code. It works once and expires in five minutes. Nobody gets in without a device you approved.
  • Remove any device instantly. Turning phone access off signs everything out and gives your address back.
  • Share links are secret and temporary. They expire after a week, can be turned off any time, and are view-only unless you allow typing.
  • Your Claude stays yours. It uses your own Claude Code login and history, on your computer.
FAQ

Good to know.

Why does macOS say it can’t check the app?

Studio CLI isn’t signed with a paid Apple developer certificate yet, so macOS asks you to approve it once. Open the app, click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Studio CLI. You won’t be asked again. Prefer Terminal? xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Studio CLI.app" does the same.

Which Mac do I need?

Any Mac running macOS 12 or later, Apple silicon or Intel. Not sure which you have? Apple menu → About This Mac. The download button picks the right file for you.

Do I need Claude Code?

Only for Claude panes. Terminals work without it. If you want Claude, install it from claude.com/claude-code and sign in once. Studio tells you if it’s missing and shows the one-line install.

Is phone access safe?

Your phone reaches your Mac through a private encrypted tunnel to an address only you have, and every phone must be paired with a single-use code you create on your Mac. You can see and remove paired devices, and turn phone access off completely at any time. Your Mac has to be on and awake for your phone to reach it.

Is it open source?

Yes. Studio CLI is MIT licensed and the code is on GitHub, so you can read exactly what it does, build it yourself, or suggest changes. The Mac app is built from that same code.

Is it free? Windows or Linux?

Free for now, and Mac only for now.

How do I update or uninstall?

To update, download the new version and drag it over the old one in Applications. Your workspaces and Claude history are kept. To uninstall, drag Studio CLI to the Trash.
